NumMerge is a small puzzle game I designed and built in Unity. Numbered balls drop into a playfield. Chain three or more consecutive numbers and they merge into a larger ball.

There is an endless Arcade mode and a Puzzle mode with authored levels. Online leaderboards track high scores.

This is a finished independent game, not a studio production credit. It is here because I still design and ship in Unity.
