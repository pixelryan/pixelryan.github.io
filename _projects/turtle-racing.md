Twitch Plays Turtle Racing is a chat-driven racing experiment. Spectators buy turtles, enter them in races, and watch odds move between events. Commands go through Twitch chat.

I talked through the Twitch integration at a local Unity meetup and left the code public. It is a finished social experiment, not a commercial racer.
