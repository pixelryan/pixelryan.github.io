Cookie Jam was a live match-3 with millions of daily players. I designed levels and live events, and I treated difficulty and economy as things you read from analytics rather than from a designer's gut alone.

### What I owned

- Levels and live events for a game that was already at daily-player scale.
- Difficulty and economy informed by player analytics — not a one-pass tune.
- Live-ops tools in the Unity editor, so event and quest work did not have to wait on a full engineering cycle.
- On Cookie Jam Blast, I iterated on mechanics and built early levels that taught the new modes.
- As a level tuner I did final review of other designers' levels so weekly content actually shipped.
- I also wrote Google Sheet formulas that took repetitive tracking off the design team's plate, and wrote design specs for live-event features that went through cross-discipline development and deployment.
