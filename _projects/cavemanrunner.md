### The original, 2011

I co-developed _Caveman Runner_ with Tom Carroll and released it for iPhone in 2011, early in the endless runner genre, before the term was common and while the App Store was still young.

It taught me to keep scope small enough to actually finish, and it taught me the full path to release: Apple's guidelines, the review process, and getting an app approved. Those lessons carried into every mobile game I've worked on since.

### Version 2.0, today

Fifteen years later, I brought the same character back through Funtownarcade Games, updated for modern iPhones. It connects where I started in mobile games with what the studio is building now.
