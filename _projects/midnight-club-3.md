This is how I got into the industry. QA on Midnight Club 3: Dub Edition — gameplay, rendering, UI, crashes, and multiplayer, including modes like capture the flag that needed long test passes.

Cruise mode, where players could roam freely, was a favorite on the floor. It is the first time I learned that a "side" mode can be the thing players actually talk about.
