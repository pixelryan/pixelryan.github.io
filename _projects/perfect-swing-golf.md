Perfect Swing Golf is an Unreal mobile golf game. I joined as Assistant Project Manager for the last stretch of development, the worldwide launch, and the live-ops period that followed.

This is the production story.

### What I owned

- Launch plans and the worldwide mobile rollout, then the update cadence after ship.
- Live ops: keeping feature work, player support, and store presence in the same conversation.
- Customer service: selected and stood up the CS ticketing system, then trained representatives across time zones so coverage was 24/7.
- Localization in 12 languages, working with translation teams and engineers so the ship was actually playable worldwide.
- App Store Optimization and featuring on both the Apple App Store and Google Play.
- Feature priority from Google Firebase — what players were actually doing, not what the backlog wished they were doing.
- QA through launch and live updates: tighter coverage, fewer surprises in the build that went out.
