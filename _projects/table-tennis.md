Table Tennis shipped in the Xbox 360 launch window. I was QA Lead.

### What I owned

- Microsoft Xbox 360 Technical Certification Requirements (TCR) through a launch-window ship — close communication with the producer, regular TCR assessment, and reporting that the rest of the team could act on.
- Focus-group testing, used to find whether the game was actually fun to play, not only whether it booted.
- Test automation with programmers, so testers could spend time on the tests that still needed a human.
