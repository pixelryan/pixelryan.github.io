I ship production and quality work: worldwide mobile launches, live ops, certification-window QA, and the tools that keep a game moving after it ships.

As Assistant Project Manager at ESTsoft I took **Perfect Swing Golf** (Unreal) through worldwide mobile launch and live ops — standing up CS ticketing and training reps across time zones for 24/7 coverage, 12-language localization, ASO and featuring on the Apple App Store and Google Play, Firebase-informed feature priority, and tighter QA through launch and live updates.

Before that I designed levels and live events for **Cookie Jam** at Jam City for millions of daily players, led **Cafe World** content QA at Zynga during a developer transition, and spent seven years at Rockstar San Diego: QA on _Midnight Club 3_, QA Lead on _Table Tennis_ through Microsoft Xbox 360 TCR, and technical design on _Red Dead Redemption_ (600+ animation state machines; mentored two QA testers into design).

Since November 2021 I have been an independent game designer and Unity developer — NumMerge, Guess Quest, Turtle Racing, and other finished games. A.A. Software Development, Irvine Valley College (2002). Unity Developer certificate (2018).
