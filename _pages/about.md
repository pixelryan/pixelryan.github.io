Hi, I'm **Ryan Dormanesh**, a game designer and Unity developer in the Pacific Northwest, and the founder of **[Funtownarcade Games LLC](https://funtownarcade.com)**, an independent studio in Washington State.

I've spent more than 20 years making games. I started in QA at **Rockstar San Diego**, then moved into technical design on _**Red Dead Redemption**_, where I built the game's animation state machines: the player's moves and weapons, multiplayer, minigames, unique moments in story missions, and the daily routines of its towns and wildlife. From there I went into live games: QA on _FarmVille_ and _Café World_ at **Zynga**, level design on _Cookie Jam_ at **Jam City** and _Tropical Treats_ at **MobilityWare**, and running live operations for _Perfect Swing - Golf_.

Those two worlds taught me two ways to push the limits. At Rockstar, I learned there's no secret: you do the hard work, and sometimes you go past what you thought was possible. In live games, I learned to push another way, changing a game quickly based on what players and the data show.

### What I'm working on now

- **[Caveman Runner 2.0](https://apps.apple.com/app/id418102156)** is out now for iPhone. The 2011 original, rebuilt for today's hardware.
- **NumMerge**, a number-chaining puzzle game, is planned for the App Store and Google Play in November 2026.
- **Treasure Drop**, **Every Card Counts Solitaire**, and **Hexa** are in development at [Funtownarcade](https://funtownarcade.com).
