# NumMerge Privacy Policy

_The official, always-current version of this policy is at [https://funtownarcade.com/nummergeprivacy](https://funtownarcade.com/nummergeprivacy)._

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

NumMerge is a puzzle game made by Funtownarcade Games LLC ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the information described here. This policy explains what the game collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

## 1. What the game collects

**For every player, to run the game:**

- An anonymous player ID. Unity Gaming Services signs you in anonymously and creates a random ID. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
- Your progress and purchases. Stars, boosters, skins, levels and the records of what you bought are saved with Unity Cloud Save, so a reinstall doesn't lose them.
- Game settings. Unity Remote Config sends the game its current settings (for example prices and level tuning). To do this it receives your player ID, app version and basic device information.

**How you choose:**

- Outside the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the first screen asks you to accept this policy with one button. It tells you that gameplay statistics, leaderboards and personalized ads are on. You can turn each one off, or tell us you are under 16, at any time in Settings > Privacy.
- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the first screen asks your age group and keeps gameplay statistics, leaderboards and personalized ads off unless you turn them on or choose "Accept All". "Reject Optional" turns them all off, and you can still play the whole game.

**When gameplay statistics and leaderboards are on (16 or older only):**

- Leaderboard scores, saved with the player name the game gives you (for example "WavySingingFlamingo"). You cannot type your own name, so no real name is shown.
- Gameplay statistics, through GameAnalytics and PostHog: levels started, won and lost, boosters used, stars earned and spent, ads watched, purchases made in the game, and session length, with a random ID the game makes on your device. These come with device information: device model, operating system, language, country (estimated from your IP address), and device or advertising identifiers.
- Install source (Android). The game reads the Google Play Install Referrer once to learn which store page or ad campaign the install came from, and records only that channel with the statistics above.
- Install source (iPhone). The game asks Apple's ad attribution service once whether the install came from an Apple Ads campaign, and records only that answer with the statistics above.
- Personalized ads. See section 3.

If you turn gameplay statistics and leaderboards off, they stay off. If you turn personalized ads off, rewarded videos are still available but are not personalized.

If you tell us you are under 16, leaderboards and gameplay statistics are off, and the rewarded videos you choose to watch are child-safe and never personalized (we ask our ad partners to treat you as a child). Otherwise only the anonymous player ID, Cloud Save and Remote Config described above are used, plus purchase receipts if you buy something (section 2).

We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

## 2. Purchases

Purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment details; we never see them. We receive a purchase receipt (product, order ID, date and price) so we can deliver what you bought and restore it later.

## 3. Ads

NumMerge shows only rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for example for extra moves. We use LevelPlay (Unity's ad mediation, formerly ironSource) to choose an ad from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. To show an ad these partners receive: your advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), your IP address, device model, operating system, language, approximate location estimated from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

- Personalized ads (when they are on): the partners may combine this with information from other apps and websites to show ads that suit your interests and to measure them.
- Non-personalized ads (when they are off, and always for players under 16): the partners still use this information to show the ad, limit how often you see the same one, prevent fraud and count views, but not to build a profile of you.

**Our partners process this information under their own privacy policies:**

- Unity Technologies (LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, Unity Gaming Services): [https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy)
- GameAnalytics ApS (gameplay statistics, processed on our behalf): [https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-faq](https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-faq)
- PostHog Inc. (gameplay statistics, processed on our behalf and stored in the EU): [https://posthog.com/privacy](https://posthog.com/privacy)

## 4. Why we use it, and our legal bases

- To run the game, keep your progress and purchases, and deliver what you buy (performance of our contract with you).
- To show rewarded videos, keep them working, and prevent fraud (our legitimate interest in funding a free game).
- Leaderboards, gameplay statistics, install source and personalized ads: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, your consent; elsewhere, your acceptance on the first screen. You can turn each off at any time in Settings > Privacy.

## 5. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your information for money. When you allow personalized ads, sharing your advertising ID and device information with ad partners may count as "selling" or "sharing" for targeted advertising under some US state laws (such as California's). Turning off "Personalized ads" in Settings > Privacy opts you out.

## 6. Your choices

- In NumMerge, open Settings > Privacy to change your age group and turn gameplay statistics and leaderboards, and personalized ads, on or off at any time. Videos stay available either way. Turning something off stops it from then on; it does not delete what was already collected.
- iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking controls whether apps may ask to track you, and whether NumMerge may.
- Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads) lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.
- You can ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing [contact@funtownarcade.com](mailto:contact@funtownarcade.com), or by tapping "Delete my data" in Settings > Privacy, which writes the email for you with your player name and IDs and turns gameplay statistics off. We answer within 30 days.
- Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device.
- If you are in the EU, the UK or another place with similar laws, you may also object to our use of your information, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

## 7. Children

NumMerge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks your age group before anything else; elsewhere you can choose Under 16 at any time in Settings > Privacy. We never ask for a birth date. Players who choose Under 16 get no leaderboards or statistics, and only child-safe, non-personalized videos. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email [contact@funtownarcade.com](mailto:contact@funtownarcade.com) and we will delete it.

## 8. Where it is stored, and for how long

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses or similar safeguards. We keep:

- progress and purchase records while you play, and delete them when you ask;
- leaderboard entries while the leaderboards run;
- gameplay statistics for up to 2 years.

Ad partners keep information as their own policies describe.

## 9. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored by our providers, who protect it with industry-standard measures.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and show the new version in the game. If a change needs your consent, we will ask again.
